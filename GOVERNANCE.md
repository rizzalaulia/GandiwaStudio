# Governance

Gandiwa Studio currently uses a benevolent-maintainer model.

## Maintainer

Rizzal Aulia Ramadhan (`@rizzalaulia`) is the initial maintainer and final decision maker for product scope, releases, security, and contributor access.

## Decision Process

Small fixes proceed through pull-request review. Significant architecture, data-model, marketplace-rule, provider semantics, deployment target, queue, license, or MVP-scope changes require an issue and an Architecture Decision Record. Decisions prioritize user safety, source-backed compliance rules, maintainability, and the locked MVP. Accepted records are indexed in `docs/adr/README.md`.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Sustained high-quality contributions, respectful collaboration, reliable reviews, and security awareness may lead to maintainer invitation. Access follows least privilege and may be revoked for inactivity or policy violations.
